Jiluka is an independent japanese visual kei band that produces electro metal music. Visual kei, or vkei bands, focus primarily on visuals and stage presence. They were formed in 2013 and restarted in 2015.

There are 4 members: sena, boogie, ricko, and zyean.

MUSIC

MEMBERS

SENA

Sena is the guitarist and leader of the band. Most of their songs are also written and composed by him.

BOOGIE

Boogie is the bassist of the band.

RICKO

Ricko is the lead vocalist of the band.

ZYEAN

Zyean is the drummer of the band. His name is pronounced not exactly, but very similarly to "Jane."

MUSIC

Jiluka's early music blends metal with electro elements and classic visual kei sounds. Their song, Screamer, released in 2015 relied heavily on aggressive blast beats and technical breakdowns.

Their songs gradually integrated more djent musical elements, which are elements of progressive metal, making their music more layered. Their EP Metamorphose is a good example of this.

Currently, their focus is on EGM music, or electro gothic metal. They began this era with their 2022 single, BLVCK.

Lots of their song covers feature similar designs and aesthetics. They have also covered a couple of songs, like Ghost Rule by Deco*27, in their cover EP, Polyhedron.
